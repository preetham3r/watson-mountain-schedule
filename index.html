Late work and retakes, by class

The rules are not the same in every class. This is the part that costs marks.

Math 7Strictest. No extra credit at all. Late work is not accepted once the related summative has been taken. No retake policy stated.
Science 6HRetake offered on any major summative scored below 70. Mrs. DiPietro emails you and Adi when one is available.
English 6HRetakes on major summatives only, at 70 or below. Minors have no retake. Formatives cannot be handed in after the summative is taken.
US HistoryFollows the LCPS Assessment and Grading Handbook. A Missed Learning Resource sits in every weekly Schoology folder.
Spectrum 6Most forgiving. Two week window to turn in anything missed. Work sits marked "missing" in Schoology until assessed, and late work is graded at the end of each week. No traditional grades and nothing appears in ParentVUE for this class, it is all in the Schoology gradebook.
Strings 6Rehearsals and concerts are graded major summatives. Planned absences need two weeks notice.

All cores weight the same: 60 percent major summatives, 30 minor, 10 formative. Missed work shows as Z in ParentVUE until the teacher grades it.

If she is absent

1Call the office on 571-367-4250 before or at the start of the day.
2Send a written or electronic reason within three school days of her return. Miss that and it is recorded as unexcused, per LCPS Policy 8140.
3Open Schoology the same evening. Every teacher posts what was covered, and Mr. Dickerson files a Missed Learning Resource weekly.
4She gets the equivalent of one block period per absent day to hand work in. Because blocks meet every other day, one missed day can mean four days of catch up.
5Tell Mrs. Holowecki separately if a rehearsal or concert is affected. Two weeks notice for anything planned.

Thresholds worth knowing: five unexcused absences triggers intervention, so does eighteen total whether excused or not, and missing more than ten percent of the year counts as chronic absence.

Worth knowing

  • The instrument travels on B and D days only. Violin students share lockers, so the violin comes home the same day it goes in. Every B and D day, both directions.
  • Blocks meet every other day. One missed day can mean four days before that class comes round again, which is why catch up compounds fast.
  • English 6H and Advisory are the only two she has daily. Everything else is every other day.
  • Grade 6 lockers are on floors 2 and 3. Chromebook exchange is in the auditorium on floor 3, supply pickup in the library on floor 2.
  • Spectrum grades never appear in ParentVUE. That class uses the Schoology gradebook only and gives no traditional grades, just rubrics and two semester self assessments. An empty ParentVUE line for Spectrum is normal, not a missing grade.
  • Spectrum has no homework by design. If she is working on it at home, she either chose a harder project or used class time poorly.
  • Mr. Dickerson sends a weekly family update by email. If one stops arriving, he asks to be told.
  • The school's own sheet for the 2:38 early dismissal contains copy errors in its lunch and passing times, so that variant is deliberately left off this page. Ring the office if it is ever called.
