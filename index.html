Worth knowing

  • The instrument travels on B and D days only. Violin and viola students share lockers, so it has to come home the same day it goes in.
  • Math is the strictest grader. No extra credit, no retake policy stated, and late work dies once the related summative is taken. Science and English both offer retakes on major summatives at 70 percent or below.
  • All cores weight the same: 60 percent major summatives, 30 percent minor, 10 percent formative.
  • Rehearsals and concerts are graded major summatives. Planned absences need two weeks notice.
  • ParentVUE's term table lists English as rotation A, B in blocks 3 and 4. That would collide with Spectrum and Strings. The section code 3/4A & 5/6B is the accurate one, and this grid follows it.
  • Grade 6 lockers are on floors 2 and 3. Mr. Dickerson sends a weekly family update by email.
